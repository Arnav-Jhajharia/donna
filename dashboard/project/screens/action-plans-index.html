Six philosophies for the action surface.

Donna's dashboard should do more than show you what she knows. It should be where she proposes the machinery she wants to install in your life, and where you accept, tune, or decline. Every action collapses to one of six AttentionSpec cards: tally, event‑stream, brief, prep‑doc, open‑loop, ping. The question is how they arrive.

Each plan below answers three questions differently: how aggressively does Donna shadow? When do offers surface? How are the four surfaces — Living, Today, Offers, Machinery — mixed on screen? Click any to open the signature moment.

How to read these

Each prototype renders the signature moment of the plan — the one screen that makes it distinct from the others. Most also show a thin layer of the Living surface at the top, because you said the dashboard is "everything Donna knows about the user," not only the action surface.

All six share the same AttentionSpec vocabulary underneath (tally · event‑stream · brief · prep‑doc · open‑loop · ping) and the same design system. They differ only on policy and surface mix.
