The Living surface, specified.

Most AI apps hide the user model. Donna shows it. The Living surface is both a trust artifact (you can see what she thinks about you) and a write path (you can correct her when she's wrong).

Rendering it as a short serif letter at the top — "what i know about aarav" — makes the model legible without feeling like a settings page. The detail rows below are editable, but the letter is the emotional artifact.

StructureFour sections: core facts (scalar values), people (relational), patterns (Donna's inferences), constitution (how the user wants her to work). Each row is editable.
ProvenanceEvery fact carries a source line — told me · 18 apr vs auto · first message metadata vs inferred · confidence 0.68. Users can trust what they can audit.
PatternsInferences show evidence and confidence. User can correct ("no, that's not why") which writes a negative example back to the pattern store.
People cadenceEach person has a stated cadence (daily/weekly/ad-hoc). Stale rows (past cadence × 1.5) turn amber. Dad is stale today.
Cold startMissing fields render as prompts inline (see "i don't know your email yet"). No separate onboarding flow — Living fills in as you use Donna.
PrivacyFooter line: "everything here can be corrected · nothing here is shared." User can delete a fact, a pattern, or a whole person. Deletions cascade into memory.
SearchLiving is also a searchable surface. ⌘K from the footer hints at this — search her notes, not just chat history.
Plan useE puts this at the top of the main scroll. F keeps it one tap deeper. D shows a one-line summary in the home header. A leads with it above the prompt. B shows it on weekdays only.
