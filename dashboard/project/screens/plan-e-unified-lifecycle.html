Why Unified Lifecycle is a serious option.

This is the most legible Donna can be. A user who wants to understand Donna as a system — not only as a voice — sees the whole shape at once. Shadow attentions are visible, which changes the emotional texture: there's no hidden brain. She's deciding where you can watch.

It's also the riskiest in a specific way: shadowing is uncomfortable to look at. "Why is she watching my mom without asking?" is a reasonable reaction. This plan has to earn that visibility with tone.

ShadowAggressive and visible. The shadow band is a real UI object, not hidden state.
PromotionAmbient. Each shadow ticks toward promotion visibly (3/7 ticks). User can promote early or archive early from the row itself.
Cold startFirst few days show an empty Live band and a full Living band that's mostly blanks the user fills in. Shadow band is empty. Teaching surface is the Living band itself.
TemplatesOne row template per AttentionStatus × AttentionCard = 6 cards × 4 states = ~24 combinations. Most collapse because Living has its own shape and Thesis is singular.
Wins whenUser is curious, high-agency, wants the system to be legible. Dashboard reads like Day One crossed with a ledger.
Loses whenOverwhelming for novices. Also: showing shadow rows can feel like "why are you watching that?" — needs careful tone.
