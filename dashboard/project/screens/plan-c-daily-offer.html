Plan C

Daily Offer — one, never more.

The dashboard always feels calm. Shadow runs aggressively, but promotion is rate‑limited to one offer per day. If Donna has ten candidates, nine wait. If she has none, the offer slot carries an invitation to author one yourself.

A middle plan — Donna earns a little each day. Users who hated being interrupted now get one small interruption at most; users who wanted Donna to notice things get Donna noticing things.

Why Daily Offer is a serious option.

This is the middle path. One tax per day, never more. It matches WhatsApp's ambient rhythm better than the weekly harvest; it respects attention better than always‑on proposals.

The constraint forces Donna to pick her best candidate — which is its own kind of quality signal. A dashboard that shows you her third‑best idea is a dashboard she's already over‑fitted to her own judgment.

ShadowAggressive. Same as Plans B, D, E, F — propose_and_shadow every 6h.
PromotionHard cap: max_offers_live = 1 per user. A new offer never appears until the current one is resolved (accepted, rejected, or carried past expiration).
QueueingShadow candidates accumulate in priority order. The highest-priority unresolved candidate is promoted daily at 07:00 local if the slot is empty.
Cold startOffer slot holds a write-prompt ("tell me about one person who matters") until shadow has a candidate. This turns the slot into a teaching surface by default.
TemplatesOne "today's one offer" template used for all six card types. Six inner variants for card bodies. Machinery view is a linked secondary screen.
