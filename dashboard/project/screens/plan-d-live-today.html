Why Live Today, Offers Deep is a serious option.

This plan is the clearest demonstration that Donna is living with you, not waiting for your attention. Every block is a running attention reporting in. There are no proposals here. The feed is the product.

Offers live where email lives — behind a badge. This is the most ergonomically familiar plan; users know what "inbox" means. It's also the plan most at risk of feeling like ten other apps.

ShadowAggressive. propose_and_shadow every 6h; promotion runs on its own rhythm.
RoutingThree routes: / (today), /offers (backlog), /machinery (garden). Unread offers count badges / but never interrupt today's scroll.
Cold startHome is empty. The three tabs all point to an onboarding flow that writes Living facts + spawns one user-authored attention to give today something to show.
TemplatesSix live-today block templates (one per card type) — this is where the best design work lives. Plus inbox row templates for /offers, machinery garden for /machinery.
Wins whenUser wants the dashboard to show them living, not ask them things. Familiar mental model.
Loses whenOffers page gets ignored. Shadow accumulates cruft. Also: each block is small real estate — rich brief or prep_doc needs a second layer.
