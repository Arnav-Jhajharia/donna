Why Invitation is a serious option.

This is the plan with the highest trust floor. Donna never surprises you. Every attention on the dashboard came from a sentence you said out loud. When she renders a dry run, it's because you started it.

It is also the least ambitious. If you expect Donna to notice things you can't, this plan trains her to never try.
