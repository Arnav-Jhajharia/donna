The garden is a shared surface.

Every plan (B through F) needs a place to browse what's currently running. This prototype is the canonical design — it's the same under every plan, with only the entry point changing (inline in E, a linked route in C/D, a secondary view in B/F).

Three metaphors: garden (biological, you tend it), shop floor (workshop, she builds things), ledger (financial, rows and numbers). The serif-heavy, sparkline-light treatment here is the garden. The ledger variant is what Plan E's main scroll uses.

SortDefault: live first (by last hit), then paused, then resolved. Filter chips reorder by card type but preserve this vertical stratification.
Row contentsIcon (card type) · title · because-line · stats (since live / hits / cadence) · optional sparkline for tally and event_stream · status tag · overflow menu (pause, retune, retire).
Aging signalOpen_loops past their stated horizon turn oxblood. The dad call at 6d shows aging because you said "this week" on tuesday — that deadline has passed.
Add affordanceBottom of the garden has one CTA — "ask me to watch something." Routes to Plan A's intent prompt regardless of which plan the user is on.
Cold startGarden starts empty. Instead of zero rows, shows a larger empty-state card: "nothing growing yet. your ritual tomorrow morning will plant the first three."
Shared templates6 machinery-row templates (one per card type), a filter-chip bar, a section divider, an empty-state card. These are the templates every plan inherits.
