Why Quiet Garden is a serious option.

Interruption is the tax; this plan pays it once a week. The rest of your week, Donna never surprises you with a proposal. That's very different from most AI surfaces, which pay the tax on every turn.

The harvest page is also a rare moment in software where Donna speaks in one voice across seven things at once. It's her weekly read of you. That read is emotionally coherent or it isn't — and the user knows.

ShadowAggressive. propose_and_shadow runs every 6 hours, pulling from chat messages, calendar patterns, observation rollups.
PromotionBatched. Sunday 19:00 local triggers run_shadow_cycle, which promotes any SHADOW with promotion_hits ≥ criteria to OFFERED. Cap: 7 offers, ranked by priority.
WeekdaysDashboard shows thesis + Living + Today (live attentions) only. Offers count is hidden or quiet (no badge).
Cold startFirst 14 days: shadow accumulates silently. Home shows "i'm learning, give me a week" card plus a writes‑to‑Living intake (name, people, season). Sunday 2 is the first real harvest.
TemplatesOne harvest‑page template + six per‑card offer rows + a batch‑commit strip. Plus the weekday home template.
Wins whenUser values low interruption, ritual rhythm, weekly reviews. Feels like the Sunday paper.
Loses whenUser opens dashboard every day expecting novelty — Mon–Sat feels static. Also: a missed Sunday = 14 stale offers next week.
